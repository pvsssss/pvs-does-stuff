I want to own a fashion brand someday. 

Ever since i passed grade 10, i have constantly been hearing "your focus should only be on your studies and NOTHING else." or say "nothing should matter to you other than studying and thinking about your future at this point of time" or say "you should stop doing anything or hanging out with anyone that takes your mind off studies". Of course as i started going to college, i have been hearing a lot less of said "advice" but i do get to hear it once in a while with a modified version of the same shit that basically boils down to "get a job mfker". And i get it, they want what's the best for me (by they i mean my parents and my older sis; should have cleared that up before but oh well) but sometimes i feel that their vision of "best" and my version of "best" don't actually coincide, i would go as far as to say that it's worlds apart, quite literally.
![[i hope i dont get humiliated.webp]]
 I want to live out my life somewhere peaceful where there arent a lotta ppl around with a nice weather and basic quality of life. What comes to mind when i imagine such a place is Japan. Basically I want to get out of this shithole of a country. I have even started learning Japanese so that by the time my graduation ends, i'll have a job in Japan. My parents on the contrary don't want to go to Japan, literally anything but Japan. Yikes. And i cant leave em here. Double yikes. 

I am more of an 'experimentalist' when it comes to life, the 'fuck it, we ball' typa guy, the 'fuck around and find out' mindset. It keeps life feeling exciting and fun. Contradictorily, my family is the complete opposite. I will explain this through an example - we recently installed new bldc ceiling fans that come with these lil remotes with a bunch of buttons on it. Naturally one would want to press each button to figure out what each button does, my mum on the other hand maid her disapproval clear of me fucking around w the remote to figure out how it works because i might *break* it. You are telling me a remote thats designed to have lil buttons on it that are **meant** to be pressed and used, will break if **i** pressed them which mind you is the intended fucking purpose of the whole goddamn remote.😀

I want to try out new things, mostly related to blue collar jobs but for all intents and purposes i think these are all very cool skills to have like - sewing, wood working, cooking, pottery, etc.
I am a very big fashion enthusiast, so much so that i someday wanna design my own clothes and create a fashion brand with my name on it. Naturally to work towards that dream, i thought the first step is to learn to sew shit and borrowed my grandma's crusty old electric sewing machine. When my mom got to know that i got the sewing machine out she got hugely upset and decided it was time for an intervention before things get too far and so like a good mother she told me that what i am doing is worthless and i would not benefit from sewing one bit, and that i should not try to become a 'tailor' and instead focus my time on better things like studying and getting a job. And so, the sewing machine is now back in it's box with my grandmother where it originally belonged. P.s. - this happened after i spent 3 hours of my life cleaning that fucker up and oiling it.

So yeah, nevertheless, i am moving out to Japan and am definitely gonna make my fashion brand someday. Thats the dream atleast. I only got one life and i am gonna live it how i want.
